Meet Smile

Your friendly Milae assistant

What Is Smile?

Smile is the friendly voice that lives in your Milae watch and phone. Just talk to it, and it helps you out, like a helpful friend that's always with you.

What Smile Can Do

❓ Answer your questions
⏰ Set alarms and timers
📞 Make calls and send messages
👣 Tell you your steps
⌚ Change your watch face

The brain behind Smile

What Is PlantAI?

PlantAI is Milae's very own AI. It's a smart AI, just like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen, and Doubao. It's built into every Milae device, and it's what makes Smile smart.

How We Got The Name

One day we saw an artificial plant, and it gave us an idea. AI means artificial intelligence, so why not name our AI after an artificial plant? That's how PlantAI was born!

Look closely at the A in our logo. Can you spot the little plant? 🌱

On Edge Watches

The Watch One Edge can use other AIs too. But PlantAI is always there, built in.
